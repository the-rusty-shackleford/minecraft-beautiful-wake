# D-0006 — Every quad on the water is wound counter-clockwise seen from above, and the booth can run under Sodium, Iris and the pack's shaders

**Decision**: The mesh's quads and the splash squares are emitted
counter-clockwise as seen from above, so their geometric normal points
up and they are front faces to a camera above the water. The booth
photographs under the pack's shaders on demand: Sodium and Iris go in
`run/booth/mods` (kept in `run/booth/shader-mods` between runs), the
pack and its settings in `run/booth/shaderpacks`, `enableShaders=true`
in `run/booth/config/iris.properties`, and the run gets
`MESA_GL_VERSION_OVERRIDE=4.6 MESA_GLSL_VERSION_OVERRIDE=460` so Sodium
accepts llvmpipe. The booth's clock waits at tick 40 until the pool's
render sections are compiled or 75 seconds pass, because a software
renderer under a shader pack runs ten ticks a frame and would photograph
stale chunks. The timing checks may trip under shaders; the photos are
the point. The booth's master volume is 20%.

**Why**: Rusty: "all of the white foamy wake pattern behind the boat
looks way different in my game than in your test booth. I suspect that
you did not run your tests with the complementary shaders." True: the
booth had only ever run the vanilla renderer. Run under Complementary
Unbound r5.8.1 with Rusty's settings, the whole mesh came out dark gray
while the bubble billboards stayed white. Complementary's entity
program (`gbuffers_entities.glsl`: `normalM = gl_FrontFacing ? normalM
: -normalM; // Inverted Normal Workaround`) flips the normal of any
back-facing fragment before lighting; the quads were wound clockwise
from above, so under the pack the sun lit them from below. Vanilla's
entity translucent type culls nothing and lights by the normal given,
so the vanilla booth could never show it. The billboards face the
camera and were front faces all along.

**Alternatives rejected**: *Emitting both windings* -- twice the
vertices for nothing; the winding is a convention to keep. *A custom
render type with its own shader* -- Iris would not know it, and the
point of the entity type is that every pack lights it.
