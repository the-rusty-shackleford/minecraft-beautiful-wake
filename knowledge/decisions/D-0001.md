# D-0001 — Client-only; the trail is geometry along sampled positions, at the Kelvin angle

**Decision**: The mod runs on the client only. Each client samples where
every visible craft is, tick by tick, into a trail, and draws the foam
strip and the two arms of the V as translucent textured quads along those
samples, right after the level's translucent blocks; spray and splashes
are particles. Speed is read off the samples over a window of six ticks.
A watercraft is any `Boat` or a configured entity id; a swimmer any living
thing with feet in the water and eyes out, not riding; a splash comes from
anything seen dry one tick and wet the next, sized by a mass judged from
what it is. The arms open at asin(1/3), Kelvin's angle, and follow the
track, each sample's point pushed out by its distance behind the bow.

**Why**: Rusty, 2026-09-08: a "beautiful, dynamic wake behind watercraft
... generically ... any watercraft going a sufficient speed", then "a touch
of wake for non watercraft as well (such as players and animals)", then
splashes for things dropped in, "an apple ... a smaller splash than ... an
iron ingot". Boat positions and speeds are already synced to every client,
so the server needs nothing and a client-only mod is the honest scope.
Geometry rather than particles for the trail because a wake is continuous
and particles at the density a trail needs are both heavier and gappier;
particles for spray because droplets are what they are. Positions rather
than the motion vector because a boat another mod moves may carry no
motion, and a remote boat's motion vector is not what the client sees.
The Kelvin angle because it is the one fact a wake has that a viewer's eye
knows without knowing it.

**Alternatives rejected**: *Particles for the trail* -- see above. *A
server component to know speeds exactly* -- nothing to gain; the client
sees the positions. *"Anything floating that moves" as a watercraft* --
swimming players and fish would be boats; the tag-free config list and
the swimmer rule cover them properly.

**Follow-ups**: shader packs may light the geometry oddly; the foam and
arms have switches. An entity type tag cannot serve a client-only mod
(tags come from the server), hence the config list.
