# D-0003 — The wake is drawn bold and cel-shaded: a floor on intensity, a solid churn over a halo, thick arms, a bow wave

**Decision**: The foam texture is bold cellular white with crisp edges,
solid down the middle and breaking into blobs at the sides, 64 by 192 so a
repeat is twenty ticks of travel. The strip starts a boat and a half wide
(`STERN_FACTOR` 1.35), is drawn twice -- a halo one and a half times as
wide at a quarter the opacity, then the solid core -- and its opacity
falls as `(1 - along)^1.5`, solid at the stern and patches a length back.
The arms are 0.9 wide at the bow tapering to 0.4 of that, at 0.95
opacity, their foam sliding back along them a little every frame. A bow
crest sits across the bow. Intensity has a floor (0.4 for a hull, 0.5 for
a swimmer) just above the minimum speed, so a paddling boat and a wading
player show a proper wake rather than a rumour of one. Swimmers are at 0.9
of a boat's strength, from a body and a half wide, reaching full at 0.14
blocks a tick, with thin arms.

**Why**: Rusty, on the first release: "there is almost no wake behind my
player when wading ... a hard to see, shitty little trail", and "the boat
wake just looks bad, like its v0.0.1 ... should resemble that from the
Zelda Windwaker games". Wind Waker's wake is bold white foam with clean
edges: an opaque churn behind the stern, thick arms from the bow, a bow
wave, all high-contrast and moving. The first release's streaky
low-contrast texture and hairline arms read as nothing at a glance; the
1.35 stern factor, the halo, the floor and the taper are the parts of the
Wind Waker look that geometry and one texture can carry. The swimmer's
"touch" at 0.45 strength with no floor vanished at a wading speed, which
is a tenth of a block a tick; it is a smaller boat's wake now, not a hint.

**Alternatives rejected**: *Particles for the churn* -- the density needed
for a solid white band is hundreds a second per boat. *A shader* -- the
pack runs Iris, whose pipeline would have to be matched; a texture and
vertex alpha work everywhere. *Keeping the swimmer faint* -- it was asked
for as a touch and rejected on sight.

**Follow-ups**: a bow wave that curls back along the hull rather than a
bar; more variety along a long straight run; the look under the pack's
shaders, which the booth cannot show.
